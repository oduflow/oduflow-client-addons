# 2026-08-20

## A quieter conversation and a chat named after its subject

- The AI bot has its own picture in Discuss instead of the default placeholder.
  An administrator can replace it on the **AI** contact; upgrading the module
  does not overwrite the replacement.
- Reasoning and tool calls that follow one another are collected into one
  collapsed group headed **AI work: `<number>` steps** with the tools it used. A
  chain of ten tool calls now takes one line instead of ten blocks, and the
  answer closes the group: the next chain starts a new one.
- Nothing in that group is expanded by default, in the stored conversation and
  in the live preview alike. The previous behaviour opened every reasoning and
  tool block while the turn was running, which scrolled the answer away by
  itself.
- Only an answer or a question addressed to you — a permission approval or a
  recovery decision — raises a notification or opens a docked chat window.
  Service steps stay silent, so one turn interrupts you once instead of once
  per step.
- A new conversation is named **OduPilot - `<your initials>`**, and the first
  answer replaces the `OduPilot` part with two to four keywords AI picked from
  your request: `Overdue invoices - MV`. A developer chat keeps its branch in
  brackets at the end of the title. Renaming the conversation by hand is still
  possible and is never overwritten afterwards.
