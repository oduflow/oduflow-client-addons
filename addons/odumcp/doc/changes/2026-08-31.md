# 2026-08-31

## Security models are readable under the global access modes

- **Read any accessible model** and **Read and update any accessible model**
  now also cover `base.automation`, `ir.actions.server`, `ir.cron`,
  `ir.mail_server`, `ir.model.access`, `ir.rule`, `ir.ui.view` and `res.groups`.
  A connector may finally look at the rules it works under — which ACL hides a
  model, which record rule narrows a list, which cron rewrites a field —
  without an explicit Model Policy per model.
- They stay strictly read-only: update, create and delete on them are refused
  in every fallback mode, exactly like `res.users`. An explicit Model Policy is
  still the only way to open one of them for writing.
- Odoo access rights are unchanged and still decide the outcome:
  `ir.model.access` and `ir.rule` remain limited to Access Rights managers, and
  `ir.ui.view`, `ir.cron`, `ir.mail_server`, `base.automation` and
  `ir.actions.server` to Settings users.
- `ir.config_parameter`, `res.users.apikeys` and the OduMCP models
  themselves are unaffected: they carry the connector's own credentials and
  audit trail and remain unreachable through any policy.
- The field filter of the fallback now also hides a bare `pass` name segment,
  so `ir.mail_server.smtp_pass` cannot be read even by a connector user who has
  the Settings right.

## Field pickers of a Model Policy stay on the policy model

- **Readable Fields** and **Writable Fields** used to open **Search More...** on
  every field of every Odoo model, so the list had to be searched by hand and a
  field of the wrong model could be picked by mistake. Both pickers are now
  restricted to the fields of the model the policy is written for.
- Because the list holds one model, granting every field of it takes two clicks:
  open **Search More...**, tick the checkbox in the header row and confirm
  **Select all N**.
