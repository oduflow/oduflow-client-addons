# 2026-08-16 — Documentation Book

## `user_guide.md`

### Added

- The change archive now offers three views instead of two: **By date**,
  **By module** and **By module & date**.
- **By date** is a chronological feed: select a month and read its changes on
  the right — days newest first, and within one day the most recently written
  entry on top — each headed by the module it applies to and by the day and time
  the change was written.

- The **Chart** button beside **Change archive** puts a chart of the months
  above the feed: one bar per month, oldest on the left, its height the number
  of changes and its upper part the unread ones. Click a bar to open that month,
  point at one to read its figures, switch the button off to hide the chart. The
  button is off when you open the view, so the feed starts without the chart. It
  is offered in the **By date** view only.

### Changed

```diff
- By date lists days grouped by month; select a day to read its entries
+ By module & date lists days grouped by month; select a day to read its entries
```

- In the month feed only the entries you scrolled to count as read; a day or a
  module is still read as a whole.

## `tech_spec.md`

### Added

- `get_changes()` entries carry `applied`, the commit time of the last commit
  that touched the entry file (file mtime only as a fallback outside git), and
  entries of one day are ordered by it, newest first.
- Commit times come from one cached `git log` per repository, with a private
  git `HOME` that allowlists the working copy under `safe.directory`.
- `read_changes(entries, mark_read=True)`, `mark_entries_read(entries)` and
  `POST /odubook/changes/read` separate rendering from marking as read.
- Described the three `ChangesApp` groupings (`date`, `module_date`, `module`)
  and the scroll observer that marks feed entries as read.
- Described the `monthChart` getter: an inline-SVG month histogram above the
  feed, unread share on top, bars calling `selectGroup`, hidden below two months
  and scrolled to its newest edge, and the `state.showChart` toggle rendered in
  the feed view only and starting as `false`.
