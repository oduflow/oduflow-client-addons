# Titles, PDF export and links in the Book

## `user_guide.md`

### Changed

```diff
- an entry is headed by the title its author wrote, date included
+ an entry is titled by what changed; the leading date is dropped, and an entry
+ without a title of its own takes the name of its module
```

- The date at the front of an entry title disappears in every view: the day is
  already shown beside the entry. An entry whose title was nothing but a date
  is titled by its module in **By date** and **By module & date**, and carries
  no title at all in **By module**, where the module name already stands above
  the group.

### Added

- Every title — an entry's own and the title of the open month, day or module —
  carries two buttons that appear when you point at it. The PDF button
  downloads everything under that title as one document; downloading does not
  mark an entry read. The link button copies a link to that title into the
  clipboard, and the link reopens the archive in the same view, on the same
  group, scrolled to the same entry and in the same language.
- Where the browser refuses access to the clipboard — over plain HTTP it always
  does — the link is shown instead, to be copied by hand.

## `tech_spec.md`

### Changed

```diff
- read_changes returns {"entries": {"<module>|<date>": html}}
+ read_changes returns {"entries": {"<module>|<date>": {"heading", "html"}}}
```

- The leading level-1 heading is split off the Markdown of an archive entry
  (`split_title`), normalized (`strip_date_stamp`) and returned separately; the
  rendered `html` no longer carries it. The pair is cached by mtime under
  `(filepath, "entry")`.

### Added

- `change_pdf(entries, title, lang)` and `GET /odubook/changes/pdf`: the
  requested entries in one self-contained HTML document rendered by
  `ir.actions.report._run_wkhtmltopdf`, answered as an attachment.
- `strip_date_stamp` / `_is_date_stamp` with `MONTH_WORDS`, `DATE_TAIL_WORDS`
  and `STAMP_SPLIT_RE`, and the `_doc_stat` / `_doc_source` split of the
  document reader.
- `ChangesApp` link parameters `group_by`, `group`, `entry` and `lang`, read
  from `props.action.params` on start, and the tool buttons beside every title.

## `user_guide.md`

### Added

- Every heading of a guide, its title included, carries the same two buttons as
  the change archive. The PDF button downloads what stands under that heading —
  the whole guide from its title, or a single section with its subsections — in
  the language you are reading. The link button copies a link to that heading,
  and the link reopens the Book on the same guide, in the same language and
  scrolled to that heading. They work in the **User Guide** and in the **Admin
  Guide** alike.

### Fixed

- A PDF no longer prints the title twice when the exported article is titled
  exactly like the cover.
- A section PDF no longer stops early when a fenced code example contains a
  language-tagged Markdown fence of its own.

## `tech_spec.md`

### Added

- `guide_pdf(module, book, section, lang)` and `GET /odubook/guide/pdf`: one
  guide or one of its sections as a self-contained PDF; `book="admin"` requires
  the administrator group.
- `split_section(text, slug)` and the public `slugify(text)` in `markdown.py`,
  and `_module_doc_path` split out of `_read_module_doc`.
- `BookApp` link parameters `page`, `section` and `lang`, read from
  `props.action.params` on start, the tool buttons appended to every rendered
  heading, and `AdminBookApp.action` / `AdminBookApp.book`.

### Changed

- `change_pdf` drops an entry heading equal to the cover title (`_same_heading`)
  instead of printing it twice; the wkhtmltopdf check moved into `_pdf_report`.
- `split_section` closes fenced code only on a marker-only fence, matching the
  renderer instead of treating a nested language-tagged opener as a closer.
